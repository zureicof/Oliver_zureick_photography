Sports Creative Portfolio

Work built around the moments people actually remember.

I’m a sports photographer and media student at Miami University with a focus on creating work that feels sharp, intentional, and honest. A lot of what I enjoy most is finding the moments that carry real emotion—whether that is the pace of a game, the pressure of a big performance, or the energy around a team and its identity.

This portfolio brings together photography, design, and digital content in one place. Some projects are built around clean game coverage. Others lean more creative through motion, graphics, and stronger visual storytelling. The goal is simple: make work that looks professional, feels real, and leaves a lasting impression.
