Featured Project One

Badin Rams 2026 Basketball

A graphic-driven project built around the Badin High School boys basketball season in Hamilton, Ohio. This body of work focuses on postseason energy, promotion, and cleaner sports design built to stand out before tip-off.

Featured Project Two

Architectural Photography

A location-based photo study centered on The Black Covered Bridge, also known as Pugh’s Mill Bridge. This set focuses on structure, texture, framing, and the relationship between the bridge and the surrounding environment.

Featured Project Three

Miami Football Gameday

A game-day storytelling project built around pregame, sideline access, player moments, fan interaction, and postgame emotion. This set shows the atmosphere around Miami football as much as the game itself.
