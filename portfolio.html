Portfolio Overview

My portfolio is built around range. I want to be able to show strong live-action work, more creative visual storytelling, and design pieces that can actually be used by teams, athletes, and media outlets.

The goal is not just to show that I can take pictures. It’s to show that I can build a body of work that feels professional, consistent, and useful in real sports media settings.
